👋 I am Qian Qiao (乔谦), an independent researcher. I received my M.Sc. and B.Eng. from Soochow University, advised by Professor Fanzhang Li. My previous research focused on multimodal understanding, image generation, text spotting, and few-shot learning.

My current interests are real-time video generation, joint video–audio generation, efficient fine-tuning, and multimodal models. I also have four years of experience in blockchain technology and investment. I am actively seeking collaborators in these fields.
